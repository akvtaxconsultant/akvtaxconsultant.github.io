Our Story

Why AKV Tax Consultant Exists

AKV Tax Consultant was founded by Ankit Vyas after 10+ years of working closely with individuals and small businesses on their taxes, GST filings and books — and seeing how often people overpaid, missed deadlines, or simply didn't know what they were entitled to claim.

The idea behind AKV is simple: give clients across Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh a consultant they can call directly, who explains things in plain language, files things correctly and on time, and treats a salaried individual's ₹50,000 refund with the same seriousness as a startup's GST audit.

Today, AKV Tax Consultant works with salaried professionals, freelancers, small business owners, startups and NRIs — handling everything from a single year's Income Tax Return to full monthly bookkeeping and GST compliance. We operate out of two offices — Gurugram, Haryana and Bikaner, Rajasthan — while serving clients remotely across Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh.
