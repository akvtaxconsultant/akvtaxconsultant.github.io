Accounting & Bookkeeping Services in North India

Monthly bookkeeping, ledger maintenance and financial statements, so your books are always audit-ready.

Clean, up-to-date books make everything else easier: tax filing, GST returns, loan applications, and knowing whether your business is actually profitable. AKV Tax Consultant provides ongoing bookkeeping and accounting support for small businesses, freelancers and startups across Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh.

Who This Is For

  • Small businesses without a dedicated in-house accountant
  • Startups that need clean books for investors or lenders
  • Freelancers and consultants who want to track income and expenses properly
  • Businesses whose books need to be cleaned up or reconciled after being neglected

What's Included

  • Monthly transaction recording and ledger maintenance
  • Bank and cash reconciliation
  • Accounts receivable and payable tracking
  • Profit & Loss statement and Balance Sheet preparation
  • Books maintained in Tally, Zoho Books, or Excel, whichever you prefer
  • Data ready for GST, TDS and income tax filing, without duplication of effort

Our Process

  • 1. Share your transactions: bank statements, invoices, bills, receipts.
  • 2. We record and reconcile: every entry categorized and matched correctly.
  • 3. Monthly reports delivered: P&L and balance sheet shared with you.
  • 4. Books stay filing-ready: feeding directly into your GST/TDS/ITR filings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need bookkeeping if I'm a small business or freelancer?
Yes, accurate books make tax filing, GST reconciliation, loan applications and business decisions much easier, and are often legally required once turnover crosses certain thresholds.
Which accounting software do you work with?
We work with commonly used platforms such as Tally, Zoho Books and Excel-based systems, and can also help set up a system if you don't already have one.
How often will my books be updated?
Typically monthly, so your financial statements and GST/TDS data are always current; weekly updates are available for higher-volume businesses.
