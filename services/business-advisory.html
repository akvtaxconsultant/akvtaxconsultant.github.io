Business Advisory & Financial Planning in North India

Entity structuring, tax planning and financial strategy for startups and growing businesses.

Beyond filings and compliance, growing businesses need someone who can help them make the right financial and structural decisions early. AKV Tax Consultant works with startups and small businesses across Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh on entity structuring, tax planning and broader financial strategy.

Who This Is For

  • First-time founders deciding how to structure their business
  • Startups planning for funding, investor due diligence, or scaling
  • Small businesses looking to optimize their tax position legally
  • Growing businesses that need a regular financial sounding board

What's Included

  • Business structure advisory: proprietorship, partnership, LLP, or private limited
  • Tax planning aligned with your income, growth stage and goals
  • Financial process setup: invoicing, payments, record-keeping from day one
  • Cash flow and profitability review
  • Ongoing advisory support as your business scales

Our Process

  • 1. Understand your business: current stage, goals, and constraints.
  • 2. Assess options: structure, tax treatment and compliance trade-offs explained clearly.
  • 3. Recommend a plan: practical next steps, not just theory.
  • 4. Support execution: registrations, filings and ongoing check-ins as needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which business structure should I choose: sole proprietorship, partnership, LLP, or private limited?
It depends on your funding plans, liability protection needs, compliance capacity, and tax outlook. We assess your specific situation and recommend the structure that fits, then help you set it up.
Can you help with startup tax planning?
Yes, we help startups plan around applicable exemptions, structure founder and investor arrangements sensibly, and set up compliant financial processes from day one.
Do you work with businesses on an ongoing advisory basis?
Yes, many clients engage us on a retainer basis for ongoing financial and tax advisory as their business grows, alongside their regular compliance filings.
