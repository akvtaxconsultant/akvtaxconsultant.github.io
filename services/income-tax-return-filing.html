Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing in North India

Accurate, on-time ITR filing for salaried individuals, freelancers, professionals and NRIs, with every eligible deduction claimed.

Filing your Income Tax Return correctly isn't just about avoiding penalties; it's about making sure you're not paying more tax than you should. At AKV Tax Consultant, we handle ITR filing for individuals and businesses across Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh, from a single salaried return to more complex filings involving capital gains, multiple income sources, or foreign income.

Who This Is For

  • Salaried individuals with Form 16 and other income sources
  • Freelancers and consultants with professional income
  • Small business owners filing under presumptive taxation or regular books
  • NRIs with rental income, capital gains, or interest income in India
  • Individuals with capital gains from stocks, mutual funds or property

What's Included

  • Review of Form 16, Form 26AS and AIS/TIS for accuracy
  • Identifying all eligible deductions and exemptions (80C, 80D, HRA, and more)
  • Correct ITR form selection based on your income profile
  • Computation of tax liability or refund due
  • Filing and e-verification of the return
  • Support in case of notices or clarifications from the department

Our Process

  • 1. Share your documents: Form 16, bank statements, investment proofs, over WhatsApp or email.
  • 2. We review and compute: we go through every applicable deduction before finalizing figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who needs to file an Income Tax Return in India?
Anyone whose total income exceeds the basic exemption limit, or who wants to claim a refund, carry forward losses, apply for a loan/visa, or meets certain other conditions, is required or advised to file an ITR: this includes salaried employees, freelancers, business owners and NRIs with Indian income.
What documents are needed for ITR filing?
Typically Form 16 (for salaried employees), bank statements, Form 26AS/AIS, proof of deductions (80C, 80D, etc.), capital gains statements if applicable, and details of any other income sources.
Can AKV Tax Consultant help NRIs file returns from abroad?
Yes. We regularly assist NRIs with Indian income sources (rental income, capital gains, or interest) to file accurate returns remotely over call, WhatsApp and email.
